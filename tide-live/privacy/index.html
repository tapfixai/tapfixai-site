Tide Live Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 September 2026

Tide Live is provided by TapFix AI Studio. Contact us about privacy at support@tapfixai.app.

Location and forecasts

Tide Live uses the location you select to request weather, marine forecasts and the local time zone. When you choose the current-location feature, Android requests your permission to access approximate or precise location. You may refuse permission and search for a place manually instead. Active location detection stops after a result, a timeout, or when the app leaves the foreground; the app does not continuously track your background location.

Selected coordinates, the requested time zone and forecast parameters are sent over HTTPS to Open-Meteo. Place names entered in search are sent to its geocoding service. These requests expose your IP address to that provider. Manually choosing a place avoids using device location but still sends that selected place's coordinates for forecasts. A saved location may be used again when you reopen the app.

Open-Meteo operates under its own privacy terms. Its published terms describe API request logging, potentially including coordinates and IP addresses, and removal of individual logs after 90 days. Provider practices may change; consult its current notice.

Data stored on your device

The app stores your selected location, including coordinates, name and time zone, and preferences such as units, character selection, lightning and haptics in app-private storage. Android manages language preferences. Clear the app's storage in Android settings to remove its local saved data. Uninstalling removes local app data, although Android backup or device transfer may retain or restore eligible settings according to your device and Google account configuration. Android backup is enabled in this version.

Accounts, advertising and analytics

This version does not require a Tide Live account and does not include advertising or third-party analytics SDKs. We do not sell personal data or use it for advertising profiling. Requests to Open-Meteo remain necessary to provide online forecasts.

Purchases

Google Play handles payment for the app under Google's terms and privacy policy. Tide Live does not ask for or receive your payment card details. Google may make purchase and order information available to the developer for payment administration, support and refunds.

Support

If you email support, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send. We use it to answer your request and resolve issues. Avoid sending unnecessary sensitive information. We retain support correspondence only as needed for support and applicable legal obligations; contact us to request deletion. Mail service providers process correspondence to deliver and store email.

Your choices and rights

You can revoke location permission in Android settings, choose another location, clear local storage, and manage Android backups in your device or Google account settings. Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict processing of personal information and to complain to a competent privacy authority. Contact support@tapfixai.app to make a request concerning information we control. Information held independently by Google or Open-Meteo is also subject to those providers' policies.

Security and international processing

Forecast requests use HTTPS and settings use app-private storage. No security measure is absolute. Google, Open-Meteo and email providers may process information outside your country. Open-Meteo describes API infrastructure in Europe and North America.

Children

Tide Live is a general weather and coastal-information utility, not a service designed to collect information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information through support, contact us so we can address it.

Changes

We will update this policy when data practices change and revise the date above. Material changes will be communicated as required by applicable law. New features will be reviewed before their data practices are described here.
