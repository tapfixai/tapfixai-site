Designed for awareness, not navigation.

Tide and marine information is presented for general coastal planning. It is not navigation-grade and must not be used for safety-critical decisions. The tide-data provider is under review, and coverage or methodology may change before release.

How online forecasts work: the current version sends the selected coordinates, requested time zone and forecast parameters to Open-Meteo over HTTPS. Place searches are sent to Open-Meteo's geocoding service, and these requests expose your IP address to the provider. Read the full Tide Live Privacy Policy.
