Questions and answers.

Where does the data come from?

Weather, waves, sea level and place search come from Open-Meteo.com, licensed under CC BY 4.0.

Are these official tide tables?

No. Tides are a modelled sea-level forecast, not official port tide tables. Do not use Tide Live for navigation or safety-related decisions.

How does location work?

Only after you tap “My location”, once, with no background tracking. You can turn it off on iPhone in Settings → Tide Live → Location, or in the app settings on Android. You can also search for a place by name or coordinates manually.

Do I need an account? Are there ads?

No account, no ads, no tracking.
